I'm Marius Henry, a PhD student at Universität des Saarlandes in Saarbrücken, Germany.

With a background in mathematics, I work on understanding large language models (LLMs). In particular, I study the theoretical properties of the underlying structures that shape their behavior, such as Transformers.
